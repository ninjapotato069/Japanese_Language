# Changelog

## 2.0.0 · 16 Nov 2026

- **All five levels.** Switch between N5, N4, N3, N2 and N1 from the level bubble in the top bar or in Settings. Each level keeps its own plan, review deck and mock scores; switching never erases anything.
- **N4 to N1 packs**: 6,900 words and 2,040 kanji from the JLPT lists, sorted by how often they are used, with example sentences, plus 132 grammar patterns with quiz items.
- **Generated mock papers**: 12 papers per level for N4 to N1, built from the real question types, including the new *usage* question type. Each section is scored out of 42, 78 and 60 points, and the pass mark is set for each level.
- **New Talk tab**: Core 1,000 words ranked by frequency, anticipation drills, five speech islands, seven survival-tactic toolkits, 16 pattern drills, ten role-play missions and a ten-minute daily workout. Core words join the review deck.
- **New look**: the current tab and level show as a filled answer bubble, there is a cleaner top bar, and phone layouts fit the screen.
- **Updates**: Settings shows the version and a “What’s new” list. The Android app can check for a new version.
- Android app renamed **JLPT Cheat Codes**; it installs over the earlier version and keeps your progress.

## 1.7

## 1.6
