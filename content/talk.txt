[tactics]
; = id | title | what it is for
; lines: Japanese | English | when to use it
= time | Buy time | Never go silent. A filler keeps your turn while you think.
ええと… | Um… | The basic thinking sound.
あのう… | Er… (to get attention softly) | Also opens a request.
そうですねえ… | Let me see… | When asked your opinion.
なんというか… | How should I put it… | When the word won't come.
ちょっと まってください。 | Just a moment, please. | To ask for real thinking time.
むずかしい しつもんですね。 | That's a hard question. | Buys several seconds politely.
= repair | Didn't catch that | Asking again is normal. Ask precisely and you sound fluent, not lost.
すみません、もういちど おねがいします。 | Sorry, once more please. | The all-purpose repeat request.
もうすこし ゆっくり はなして いただけますか。 | Could you speak a little more slowly? | Polite and specific.
「〜」は どういう いみですか。 | What does "〜" mean? | Repeat the word you missed.
〜って、なんですか。 | What's 〜? | Casual version.
えいごで なんと いいますか。 | How do you say it in English? | Last resort.
すみません、ききとれませんでした。 | Sorry, I couldn't catch that. | When it was speed, not vocabulary.
= check | Check you understood | Repeat back what you think you heard. It confirms and buys time.
〜と いうことですか。 | Do you mean 〜? | Put your guess before it.
つまり、〜ですね。 | In other words, 〜, right? | Summarise in simple words.
あした、ですね。 | Tomorrow, right? | Echo the key word + ですね.
わかりました。 | Understood. | Close the loop.
なるほど、そういう ことですか。 | I see, so that's how it is. | Shows you followed.
= aizuchi | Sound like a listener | Japanese listeners respond constantly. Silence reads as not listening.
はい。／ええ。 | Yes. / Uh-huh. | Means "I'm listening", not always "I agree".
そうですか。 | Is that so. | Falling tone: interest. Rising: doubt.
そうですね。 | That's right. | Agreement.
なるほど。 | I see. | Understanding something new.
へえ、すごいですね。 | Wow, that's great. | Positive surprise.
ほんとうですか。 | Really? | Surprise.
たいへんですね。 | That sounds tough. | Sympathy.
いいですね。 | That's nice. | Approval or envy.
= around | Say it another way | You know less vocabulary than you need. Describe around the gap.
〜みたいな ものです。 | It's something like 〜. | Compare to a word you know.
〜の はんたいです。 | It's the opposite of 〜. | おおきい の はんたい = small.
〜する ときに つかう ものです。 | It's a thing you use when you 〜. | Describe by function.
〜の なかまです。 | It's in the same family as 〜. | Category.
にほんごで なんと いうか わかりませんが… | I don't know what it's called in Japanese, but… | Then describe it.
かんたんに いうと、〜です。 | Put simply, it's 〜. | Simplify your own sentence.
= steer | Steer the talk | Move the conversation to ground you know, and hand the turn back.
〜さんは どうですか。 | How about you, 〜? | The simplest way to return the ball.
ところで、〜 | By the way, 〜 | Change of topic.
そういえば、〜 | That reminds me, 〜 | Soft change of topic.
さっきの はなしですが… | About what we said earlier… | Go back to safe ground.
それは ちょっと わかりませんが、〜なら わかります。 | I'm not sure about that, but I do know about 〜. | Redirect to your island.
もっと おしえてください。 | Please tell me more. | Makes them talk while you rest.
= close | Open and close | Start and finish cleanly.
すみません、ちょっと いいですか。 | Excuse me, do you have a moment? | Opening with a stranger or colleague.
おいそがしい ところ、すみません。 | Sorry to bother you when you're busy. | Polite opening.
きょうは ありがとうございました。 | Thank you for today. | Closing.
また こんど ゆっくり はなしましょう。 | Let's talk properly another time. | Friendly close.
そろそろ しつれいします。 | I should be going. | Leaving.
おさきに しつれいします。 | Excuse me for leaving first. | Leaving work before others.
