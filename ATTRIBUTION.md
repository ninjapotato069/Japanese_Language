# Data sources

Work in progress. The level packs in `app/data/levels/` are built by `tools/build_levels.py` from:

- JLPT word lists: [open-anki-jlpt-decks](https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks) (MIT), based on tanos.co.uk lists (CC BY)
- Kanji: [kanji-data](https://github.com/davidluzgouveia/kanji-data) (MIT), from KANJIDIC (CC BY-SA 4.0, EDRDG)
- Example sentences: Tanaka Corpus via [small_parallel_enja](https://github.com/odashi/small_parallel_enja) (CC BY)
- Parts of speech and glosses: JMdict (CC BY-SA 4.0, EDRDG)
- Word frequencies: [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) (CC BY-SA 4.0 data)
